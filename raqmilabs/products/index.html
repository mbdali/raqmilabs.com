How the Platform Connects

When an employee clocks in via Raqmi Attendance, their teller window opens on Raqmi Queue. As they serve tickets, invoices are issued via Raqmi ERP, and the customer rates the visit on Raqmi Happiness. Data moves seamlessly with zero manual re-entry.
