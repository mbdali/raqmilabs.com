Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective version 1.0

1. Overview & Commitment

Raqmi Labs ("Raqmi Labs", "we", "our", or "us") provides modular SaaS enterprise applications (including Raqmi ERP, Raqmi Queue, Raqmi Attendance, and Raqmi Happiness) and specialized IT engineering services. We are dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data protection, privacy, and sovereignty for our clients, their personnel, and end citizens.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide our services and respond to inquiries:

  • Inquiry & Consultation Information: Full name, professional email address, organization name, phone number, and inquiry messages submitted via our direct communication channels.
  • Client Operational Data: Data processed within our licensed SaaS applications (e.g. employee attendance records, queue ticket dispatch logs, inventory ledgers) is processed solely on behalf of the customer under a designated Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized server access logs including IP addresses, browser types, and HTTP request headers necessary for cybersecurity, DDoS mitigation, and system diagnostics.

3. Use of Cookies and Local Storage

Our public marketing website operates with zero third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling pixels. We use standard browser localStorage strictly to remember your visual display preferences (such as light or dark theme mode) between browsing sessions. No personally identifiable information is stored within this mechanism.

4. How We Utilize Your Information

We utilize collected data solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To schedule technical demonstrations and consultations requested by you.
  • To fulfill contractual obligations and provide client technical support.
  • To maintain application reliability, prevent abuse, and enforce service boundaries.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or operational data to any third party.

5. Data Sovereignty & Storage

For enterprise and public sector customers, Raqmi Labs supports in-country sovereign cloud hosting and on-premise private deployments. This guarantees that all operational data, employee information, and citizen queue metrics remain strictly within the geographic jurisdiction required by local regulatory frameworks.

6. Security Measures

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest for database volumes, granular role-based access control (RBAC), and automated vulnerability testing.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal contact information held by us. If your organization is a licensed client seeking to exercise data controller rights under applicable regional privacy regulations, please contact our data governance team.
